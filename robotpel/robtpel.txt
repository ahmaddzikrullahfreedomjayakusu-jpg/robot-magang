Tiap kali mau MAPPING ruangan baru
HP/Android — nyalain stm_bridge.py
Terminal 1 (laptop):

"/home/freedom/Documents/Robot magang/robotpel/scripts/start.sh" mapping
Terminal 2 (laptop):

rviz2 -d "/home/freedom/Documents/Robot magang/robotpel/rviz/mapping.rviz"
Gerakkan robot manual keliling ruangan sampai map lengkap
Terminal 3 — simpan map:

source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash
ros2 run nav2_map_server map_saver_cli \
    -f "/home/freedom/Documents/Robot magang/robotpel/maps/room" \
    --ros-args -p save_map_timeout:=5.0
    
Ctrl+C di Terminal 1
